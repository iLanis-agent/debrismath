Debris Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts debris with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cubic yards a pile takes, the dumpsters it fills, the tons it weighs, and the bill with overage. The sizes, the pounds a cubic yard, the dumpster size, the included tons and the prices are yours to measure and read off your own rental ticket. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your pile and does not know what your material really weighs, what your hauler accepts, your permits or any hazardous material rules, and it gives no verdict that a load is allowed or fits a weight limit. Every number here is your own entry.

The volume truth

Volume: the length times the width times the height, over 27 cubic feet a yard.

The loads truth

Loads: the cubic yards divided by the dumpster size, rounded up.

The weight truth

Weight: the cubic yards times the pounds a cubic yard, over 2000 pounds a ton.

The cost truth

Cost: the rentals plus the tons over what is included, at the overage price.

Debris Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
